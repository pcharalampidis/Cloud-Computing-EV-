DETAILED DESCRIPTION  
A company operating a network of public EV charging stations wishes to modernise the way drivers discover  
charging stations, inspect availability, and reserve charging slots. At present, the company relies on fragmented  
tools and manual administration. This leads to poor visibility of availability, double bookings, and difficulty  
managing the system as the number of users and charging stations grows.  
Your team has been assigned the task of designing and developing a cloud-ready EV charging station  
booking system.  
More specifically, the system should authenticate users based on existing usernames and passwords. Once  
authenticated, a driver should be able to browse charging stations, inspect available connectors and charging  
slots, and reserve an available slot for a given date and time. A driver should also be able to modify or cancel  
their own reservation before the reserved charging slot begins.
